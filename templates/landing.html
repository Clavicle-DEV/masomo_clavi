For schools

Your school, on Clavis.

Register your school, approve your students, and run live study sessions. Then keep the conversation going afterwards, safely, inside your school.

  1. Register your schoolSend a request. The Clavis team reviews and approves it.
  2. You become the school adminPrincipals get their own dashboard to manage the whole school.
  3. Students find & request to joinThey search for your school. You accept or reject each request.
  4. Teach live, discuss afterTeachers host study sessions, then students discuss, only with their school.
